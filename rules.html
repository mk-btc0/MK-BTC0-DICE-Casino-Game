The 20 Official Rules

Every regulation, refined to guarantee fair play and maximum excitement.

Section I — Core Mechanics

Fundamentals & Dice Duel

01Initial Capital — Each participant begins with exactly 1,000 chips.
02Minimum Stakes — Table minimum wager per round: 10 chips.
03Maximum Ceiling — Table maximum cap per round: 100 chips.
04Wager Confirmation — Lock in your bet before dice release.
05Casino Engine Roll — Dealer rolls one unbiased die (1–6).
06Player Hand Roll — You roll an identical 6-sided die (1–6).
07Victory Condition — Your number higher than the casino's → you win.
08Defeat Condition — Your number lower → house takes the bet.
09Tie Protection — Identical values = Standoff. Full bet returned.
10Standard Payout — Every declared win awards an instant x2 return.
Section II — Session & Bonuses

Streaks, Loans & Cashout

11House Claim — On defeat, 100% of the stake transfers to the house.
12Zero-Penalty Standoff — Ties incur no rake. Wager stays untouched.
13Uncapped Rounds — Play unlimited consecutive rounds.
14Insolvency Threshold — Balance = 0 without loan → game terminates.
15Discretionary Cashout — Cash out your chips at any round break.
16Streak Reward Bonus — Every 3 wins in a row: +50 chip bonus.
17Emergency Loan Facility — After 3 losses: one-time 100 chip loan.
18Automated Repayment — 10% of gross wins services active loans.
19Session Termination — Play until voluntary exit or insolvency.
20Table Etiquette — Play responsibly. May fortune favor the bold! 🍀
